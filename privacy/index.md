# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

**Filipe Saulo Dev** ("the App") is a personal tool, used by one person, that uploads that person's own short videos to their own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

- **Who uses it.** Only its owner. The App has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.
- **What it accesses.** After the owner authorizes the App on TikTok, it receives an access token for the owner's own account, used only to upload the owner's own videos and captions.
- **Where data is stored.** The access token and the videos stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the App, because the App has none.
- **Sharing.** No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties, other than the videos and captions the owner chooses to post to TikTok.
- **Revoking access.** The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings, under the list of authorized apps.
- **TikTok's policy.** TikTok handles the posted content under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).
